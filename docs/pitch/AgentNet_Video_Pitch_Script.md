# AgentNet — Video Pitch Script
**Alchemist Silkroad · Silicon Valley Residency Application**
Target length: ~90 seconds (read at a natural, slightly slower-than-conversational pace). Word count ≈ 230.

## How to use this

Read it aloud once cold, then time yourself. If you run over 100 seconds, cut the bracketed optional lines first — they're marked `[optional]`. Look at the camera, not the script, for the Ask and Closing lines — that's the part reviewers remember.

## Script

**[0:00–0:12] Hook**

"Two billion Muslims worldwide manage their health, prayers, money, and business across ten different apps — and not one of them is built around their values. I'm [Your Name], and with my co-founder we're building AgentNet: a no-code platform where anyone can create AI agents for their life and business, with a halal filter built into every single one.

**[0:12–0:30] Problem**

Right now, a shop owner who wants AI to watch his store cameras, track inventory, and manage his books has to stitch together five different tools — none of them aware of riba, gambling content, or basic religious red lines. Generic automation platforms like n8n or Dify don't solve this. They're not built for this audience.

**[0:30–0:50] Solution**

AgentNet lets anyone — no coding — describe what they want in chat, or drag and drop on a canvas, and get a working AI agent: for prayer times and Qur'an study, for mental wellness, for store security, for family budgeting. Every agent, every input and output, passes through the same Halal Filter automatically. It's not a plug-in — it's the foundation.

**[0:50–1:05] Why now [optional if running long]**

The global Islamic economy is projected to reach $3.56 trillion by 2029. Islamic finance assets are growing past $9.7 trillion. There is real, fast-growing demand — and right now, no mainstream no-code AI platform is built for it.

**[1:05–1:20] Stage and team — honest framing**

We're two people, today at idea and early-build stage. We've used Claude to architect the full technical stack and build working code skeletons — authentication, the multi-agent engine, the halal filter, camera and bank integrations. We're not asking you to believe in traction we don't have yet. We're asking you to believe in the problem and watch us build the proof.

**[1:20–1:30] The ask**

What we need isn't capital we're raising right now — it's compute resources to run and test multi-agent workloads at scale, and mentorship from people who've taken a platform like this from zero to real users.

**[1:30–1:32] Close**

We think AgentNet can be the AI layer for two billion people's everyday life — built right, from day one. Thank you."

## Delivery notes

- Say your real name and your co-founder's role out loud in the hook — reviewers are screening for real founders, not a voiceover.
- Don't round 2.0B or the market figures up — they're already strong as-is (sourced from Pew Research and DinarStandard's 2025/26 report).
- The "stage and team" paragraph is the most important line in the whole script: it's the one place you get ahead of the obvious question ("what's actually built?") before they ask it.
- If asked to extend to 2 minutes, add one concrete example: walk through one agent end-to-end (e.g., "a shop owner says 'watch my back room camera and tell me if something looks like theft' — and AgentNet builds that agent in one chat message").
